The conditional model is coherent under its documented assumptions:
monotone sample knots displaced by at most U define a linear time warp
whose displacement is at most U. The nominal trace is Lipschitz with slope
bound L, so the indicated-power difference is bounded by LU. Applying
relative meter error to that shifted indication gives the `(1+r)LU` term.
The absolute and interpolation bounds integrate over the interval duration;
the two displaced boundaries are covered by the declared maximum-power
envelope. Expanded coverage and unambiguous knot ordering are necessary.
The synthetic numerical checks exercise this reasoning; they cannot validate
the physical assumptions for an actual device.

Strict metadata fields, duplicate/nonfinite rejection, status/gap/coverage
